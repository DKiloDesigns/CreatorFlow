## 3. Functional Requirements: The Engine Behind Your Empire

These are the nuts and bolts, the core capabilities that power your `floai.studio` experience and ensure you have all the tools you need to build and monetize your creative empire. Every functional requirement is designed to be robust, intuitive, and directly supportive of your goals as a creator:

### 3.1 Account Management: Connect Your Empire

*   **FR01: Seamless Multi-Platform Connection:** Users shall be able to connect and disconnect their social media accounts (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter/X) via secure OAuth authentication, adhering to each platform's API Terms of Service. This ensures creators can manage their entire digital presence from one central hub, without the hassle.
*   **FR02: Ironclad Security for Your Assets:** The system shall securely store user credentials and access tokens, complying with relevant security best practices. Your valuable accounts and data are protected with industry-leading security, so you can focus on creating.
*   **FR03: Unified Account Overview:** Users shall be able to view a clear list of their connected accounts with their status and easily manage them. The UI should utilize our defined clean and minimalist color palette for clarity and hierarchy, making it simple to keep tabs on your entire empire.

### 3.2 Content Scheduling & Publishing: Master Your Content Flow

*   **FR04: Dynamic Post Creation:** Users shall be able to create new posts with text, images, and videos for each supported platform, adhering to platform-specific requirements (character limits, aspect ratios, etc.). Our vibrant accent color should highlight key action buttons (e.g., "Schedule," "Publish"), making your content creation process intuitive and powerful.
*   **FR05: Precision Scheduling:** Users shall be able to schedule posts for specific dates and times in the future. This ensures your content drops when your audience is most active, maximizing visibility and engagement – a critical factor for monetization.
*   **FR06: Intuitive Content Calendar:** Users shall be able to view all scheduled posts in a clear, organized calendar view, categorized by date and time across different platforms. Our clean and minimalist design ensures you always have a bird's-eye view of your content strategy, helping you plan for optimal impact and paid opportunities.
*   **FR07: Flexible Content Management:** Users shall be able to easily edit or delete scheduled posts. Life happens, and your content strategy needs to be agile. Adjust on the fly to keep your content fresh and relevant.
*   **FR08: Reliable Automated Publishing:** The system shall automatically publish scheduled posts to the respective social media platforms at the designated times, respecting API rate limits. Your content will go live without a hitch, freeing you up to focus on creating more killer content (and earning more cash).
*   **FR09: Smart Caption & Hashtag Library:** Users shall be able to save and reuse captions and hashtag groups. This saves valuable time and ensures brand consistency, allowing you to quickly deploy optimized content and maintain your unique voice.

### 3.3 Media Library: Your Content Arsenal, Organized

*   **FR10: Centralized Media Hub:** Users shall be able to upload and store images and videos in a personal media library. Our clean and organized UI, utilizing the base colors for layout, ensures your creative assets are always at your fingertips.
*   **FR11: Intelligent Media Organization:** Users shall be able to organize their media library with folders or tags. This means no more digging for that perfect visual – keep your content arsenal neatly categorized and ready for deployment.
*   **FR12: Instant Content Preview:** Users shall be able to preview media files before using them in posts. Ensure your visuals are on point before they hit the feeds, maintaining your high-quality brand image and engagement.

### 3.4 Analytics & Reporting: Your Data-Driven Edge

*   **FR13: Actionable Content Performance Metrics:** The system shall retrieve and display key performance metrics for published posts (views, likes, comments, shares, saves – platform dependent), adhering to API data usage policies. Our vibrant accent color will highlight key trends in data visualizations (charts, graphs), giving creators the insights to optimize their content strategy for maximum engagement and monetization.
*   **FR14: Track Your Empire's Growth:** The system shall display audience growth trends (follower/subscriber count over time) for each connected platform. Easily visualize your influence expanding, providing concrete proof of your value to brands and unlocking higher earning potential.
*   **FR15: Precision Engagement Analysis:** The system shall calculate and display engagement rates for individual posts and overall profiles. Understand what truly resonates with your audience, enabling you to fine-tune your content and attract more lucrative partnerships.
*   **FR16: Flexible Data Filtering:** Users shall be able to filter analytics data by date range and platform. Get a granular view of your performance, allowing for strategic adjustments and targeted content planning.
*   **FR17: Intuitive Visual Reporting:** The system shall present analytics data in clear and understandable visualizations (charts, graphs), maintaining a minimalist aesthetic. Complex data made simple, so you can quickly grasp trends and make informed decisions to boost your bottom line.

### 3.5 Brand Collaboration Management (MVP - Basic): Elevate Your Partnerships

*   **FR18: Streamlined Collaboration Logging:** Users shall be able to log details about their brand collaborations (brand name, campaign name, deadlines, deliverables). Keep all your partnership info in one place, so you're always organized and ready to secure the next big deal.
*   **FR19: Integrated Campaign Tracking:** Users shall be able to link specific scheduled/published posts to a particular brand collaboration. This ensures seamless tracking of your sponsored content, making reporting a breeze and proving your value to brands.
*   **FR20: Basic Performance Insights for Partners:** Users shall be able to track basic performance metrics (e.g., engagement on sponsored posts) for their collaborations. Show your partners the impact of your work with real data, leading to more lucrative future engagements.

### 3.6 Notifications: Stay Ahead of the Game

*   **FR21: Timely Publication Alerts:** Users shall receive notifications upon successful publication of scheduled posts. Our clear and concise notification UI, potentially using the accent color for emphasis, ensures you're always aware when your content hits the feeds.
*   **FR22: (Future Consideration) Proactive Performance Alerts:** Users may receive notifications for significant performance milestones or potential issues. Imagine getting a heads-up when your content is crushing it or when something needs your attention – always keeping you in the loop for maximum impact.

### 3.7 User Onboarding and Support: Your Fast Track to Success

*   **FR23: Intuitive Onboarding Journey:** The system shall provide a clear and intuitive onboarding flow for new users, guiding them through account connection and basic feature usage. Our clean and minimalist design ensures you get started quickly, so you can focus on building your empire from day one.
*   **FR24: Comprehensive Support & Resources:** The system shall provide access to helpful documentation and FAQs within the application or via a dedicated support portal, maintaining a consistent visual style. Master the platform with ease and keep that money flowing, supported by our robust knowledge base.

## 8. Development Processes and Tools (Recommendations): Building the Future of Creator Monetization

To bring `floai.studio` to life with the speed, quality, and scalability required to empower creators, we recommend the following development processes and tools. These choices are driven by our commitment to delivering a robust, maintainable, and continuously evolving platform that supports your creative and financial aspirations:

### 8.1 Development Process

*   **Agile/Scrum:** We'll adopt an agile development methodology to ensure rapid iteration, continuous feedback, and a flexible approach to feature delivery.
*   **Version Control:** Utilize Git for version control, enabling multiple developers to work on the same codebase simultaneously. GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket will serve as our primary repository.
*   **Code Reviews:** Implement a robust code review process to catch potential bugs, security issues, and ensure consistent code quality across the team.
*   **CI/CD:** Leverage modern CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, Vercel) to automate testing, deployment, and monitoring. This ensures a smooth deployment process and quick feedback on new features.

### 8.2 Project Management and Collaboration Tools

*   **Task Management:** Utilize Jira, Trello, Asana, or Linear for task tracking, project organization, and team collaboration.
*   **Communication:** Slack and Discord will serve as our primary communication channels for team discussions, code reviews, and quick updates.
*   **Documentation:** Notion, Confluence, and GitBook will be our go-to tools for comprehensive documentation, API specifications, and onboarding guides.

### 8.3 Technology Stack

*   **Frontend Framework:** Next.js will be our primary choice for the frontend, offering excellent performance, server-side rendering, and a robust ecosystem for state management and routing.
*   **UI Library:** Tailwind CSS will be our choice for highly customizable styling within the minimalist aesthetic, allowing for quick and efficient development. Material UI/MUI will provide a structured component library for consistent design patterns. Chakra UI is another excellent accessible option.
*   **State Management:** Zustand or React Context API with useReducer will be our state management solution, depending on the complexity of the application.
*   **Backend (if separate):** Node.js (Express.js/NestJS) or Python (Django/Flask) will serve as our backend, handling API routes, data processing, and integration with social media platforms. Next.js API routes will be sufficient for the MVP.
*   **Database:** PostgreSQL or MongoDB will be our database of choice, providing robust scalability and reliability for storing user data, scheduled posts, and analytics.
*   **Task Scheduling:** node-cron or cloud-based solutions (e.g., AWS Lambda, Google Cloud Functions) will be used for automated post publishing and analytics processing.
*   **Authentication:** NextAuth.js will be our authentication solution, ensuring secure OAuth integrations with social media platforms and user management.
*   **Media Storage:** AWS S3, Google Cloud Storage, or Cloudinary will be our media storage solution, handling image and video uploads, ensuring high availability and scalability.

### 8.4 Testing Tools

*   **Unit Testing:** Jest and Mocha will be our unit testing frameworks, ensuring individual components and functions work as expected.
*   **Integration Testing:** React Testing Library will be our choice for integration testing, verifying that different components and features interact correctly.
*   **End-to-End Testing:** Cypress and Playwright will be our end-to-end testing tools, simulating real user flows and ensuring the entire application performs as intended.
*   **Browser Developer Tools:** Comprehensive browser developer tools (Chrome DevTools, Firefox Developer Edition) will be available for debugging and performance optimization.

### 8.5 Error Logging and Monitoring

*   **Sentry:** Sentry will be our primary error logging and monitoring solution, providing real-time alerts for critical errors, performance issues, and user feedback.
*   **LogRocket:** LogRocket will be used for session replay, allowing developers to see exactly what users are doing in the application, helping identify and fix bugs quickly.
